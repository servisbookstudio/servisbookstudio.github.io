Workshop Management App

Workshop Management,
Simplified

ServisBook keeps your service records, clients, appointments and reminders in one place — all stored privately on your device.

Get it on Google Play
Free

Everything you need

Core tools for running your service business, at no cost.

📋
Service Records
Three-status workflow: Scheduled → In Progress → Completed. Color-coded cards, search and sort by date, price or client.
👥
Client Management
Client database with full service history, appointment count and total earnings. Import directly from phone contacts.
📅
Calendar
Monthly overview and daily timeline with free and busy slots. Tap a free slot to book a new appointment instantly.
🔔
Reminders
5 types: Call, SMS, Offer, Schedule next service, Personal note. One tap opens phone, SMS or email app.
🔍
Search & Filter
Real-time search by client name or description. Filter by date range and workstation.
💬
SMS Confirmation
Send appointment confirmation or cancellation SMS to clients directly from the app with one tap.
📄
PDF & Share
Generate a service details PDF with your company data and share it via email, messaging or print.
🌐
10 Languages
English, Serbian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Croatian.
