User Guide

Step-by-step instructions for every feature of ServisBook

Contents
🚀 Getting Started 📋 Service Records ➕ Booking a Service 🔄 Statuses and Cancellation 🧾 Premium Service Details ⭐ 👥 Clients 📅 Calendar 🔔 Reminders 📊 Statistics (Premium) ⚙️ Settings and Account
1Language Setup
On first launch, select your preferred language. You can also change the language later in Settings.
2Creating an Account
Enter a username and password (minimum 4 characters). No registration required — all data is stored locally on the device.
3Company Information
Right after the first login the app takes you to the company information screen. Fill in the name, address, tax number and other required details.
4Workstations
In Settings → Company Info → Workstations add counters, booths or positions (e.g. \"Counter 1\", \"Lift\"). They allow viewing occupancy per station.
5Working Hours and Holidays
Set working hours for each day of the week and add public holiday dates. The app uses working hours when calculating service duration and displaying available slots.
6Hints
The app explains itself as you go. A tip above the service list shows the three steps, and a dimmed button tells you why it cannot be used yet. Close the tip with × when you no longer need it — it will not come back.
1Three Tabs — Three Statuses
Service records are divided into three tabs:
• Scheduled — appointments yet to be carried out
• In Progress — services currently underway
• Completed — archive of all finished services
2Time Period for Completed Services
Select the review period: 15 days, 30 days, 6 months, 12 months or all time.
3Card Colors
Cards can be color-coded:
• Yellow background — service should have started but hasn't
• Green background — service is in progress and completion time is approaching
4Sound Alarm
If you enable sound alerts in Settings → Notifications, the app will notify you with sound and vibration when a scheduled appointment time arrives.
5Search
At the top of the screen enter part of the service description or client name. The list filters in real time.
6Filtering and Sorting
Use the calendar icon to filter by date. Use the sort icon to order the list by date, price or client name.
7Daily Revenue
In the Completed tab each day shows the total earned that day. A service counts on the day it was finished, not the day it was booked.
1Starting
Tap the + button in the bottom right of the Services screen, or tap a free slot in the Calendar.
2Selecting a Client
Choose a client from the list or add a new one directly on the form. The client must be added before booking a service.
3Date and Time
Select start date and time. Enter duration or manually select end time. Duration is calculated in working hours according to your schedule.
4Description and Notes
Service description is required in Basic entry mode. Notes are optional and appear in italic on the card.
5Photos
Add photos from the gallery or camera. They are stored locally and displayed as thumbnails on the card.
6SMS Confirmation to Client
Enable the \"Send SMS confirmation to client\" toggle before saving. The app opens the SMS app with a pre-filled number and message.
7Reminder
Enable \"Add reminder\" and select the type. The reminder appears in the Reminders section on the selected date and time.
1Starting a Service
On the card in the \"Scheduled\" tab tap the \"In progress\" button. The service moves to the In progress tab.
2Completing a Service
On the card in the \"In progress\" tab tap \"Completed\". Enter the final price (if not already entered). The service moves to Completed. The \"Completed\" button only works once the service is in progress.
3Cancellation
Tap \"Cancel\" on the card in the Scheduled tab. Confirm the cancellation and, if desired, send an SMS notification to the client.
4View and PDF Export
Tap the completed service card for a detailed view. Use the share icon in the top right to generate and send a PDF.
1Enabling Premium Entry Mode
Go to Settings → Entry Mode → Premium. Available only to Premium plan users.
2Adding Line Items
In Premium mode the service form shows a table. Add rows with item name, unit, quantity and unit price.
3Tax (VAT)
Enter the VAT rate in Settings (e.g. 20%). The app automatically calculates the tax amount and total.
4PDF Service Details
On the Service Details screen tap the share icon. A PDF is generated with company data, client and all line items.
1Adding a Client
Tap + and manually enter last name, first name and phone number (required). E-mail, address and notes are optional.
2Import from Contacts
On the form tap the contact icon. The app opens the phone contacts — select a contact and the data is filled in automatically.
3Client History
Tap on a client in the list to see all their services, total number of appointments and total earnings.
4Deleting a Client
On the client profile tap the delete icon. Warning: deleting a client also removes all their services and reminders.
5Contacting a Client
Tap the phone icon next to a client in the list, or open the client and use the icons on their card. Choose call, SMS or e-mail and the app opens the matching app with the client already filled in.
6Client Limit ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Premium
The free plan supports up to 40 clients. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited clients.
1Monthly View
Shows which days have scheduled appointments. Tap a day to see the detailed daily view.
2Daily Timeline
Vertical view of the day with all scheduled appointments. Each block shows client name, description and duration.
3Booking from Calendar
Tap a free slot in the daily view to immediately open the new service form with a pre-set date and time.
4Filtering by Workstation
If you have multiple workstations, use the filter at the top to see appointments for one station only.
1Reminder Types
• Call — opens the phone app
• SMS — opens SMS app with a pre-defined message
• Offer — opens e-mail app
• New service — opens the booking form
• Personal note — personal reminder, no automatic action
2Adding a Reminder
Tap + for a new reminder. Select type, client, date and time. The reminder appears in the list on the scheduled date.
3Marking as Done
After completing the action (call, SMS...) mark the reminder as done. It moves to the Done section.
1Premium Feature
Statistics are available exclusively to Premium plan users. The free version shows a blurred preview with a lock icon.
2Available Charts
• Revenue in selected month
• Monthly revenue trend
• Revenue by workstation
• Top 5 clients by revenue and job count
• Service status distribution
• Job count trend
• Busiest day of the week
• New clients per month
• Average service value trend
• Loyal and one-time clients
• Average service duration
• Revenue details: avg, max, yearly comparison
• Completed jobs by day
1Company Information
Company name, address, tax number, registration number, bank account and contact details. Used on the service details page. Here you can also configure the working hours schedule by day, holiday dates, and workstation names.
2Workstation Limit ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Premium
The free plan supports up to 2 workstations. Upgrade to Premium for up to 20 workstations.
3Language and Theme
10 languages available. Choose between light, dark or system theme.
4Data Backup (Premium)
Create a backup of all data. The file is saved to the Downloads folder and can be used to transfer to another device.
5Data Restore (Premium)
Import a previously created backup. Warning: restore replaces all existing data in the app.
6No Ads ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Premium
The free plan shows banner ads. Upgrade to Premium to remove all ads from the app.
7Premium Plan
Upgrade to Premium for statistics, backup, line-item service view with VAT and unlimited clients and services. If you already have Premium, Manage subscription opens it in the Play Store.
8Change Password
In Settings tap \"Change password\". Enter the old and new password (minimum 4 characters).
9Restore Purchases
After reinstalling the app, or on a new phone with the same Google account, Premium comes back on its own. If it does not appear, tap Restore purchases.
10Recovery Code
The recovery code is the only way back in if you forget your password. Write it down on paper or keep it in a password manager. You can view it or generate a new one under Settings → Account.
